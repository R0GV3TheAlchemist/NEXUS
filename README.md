# NEXUS

NEXUS is the Universal Operating System architecture formed from the Super-Simulation. It is designed to support analysis, governance, and coordination across every subject of knowledge, while remaining grounded in explicit rules, measurable effects, and ethical constraint.

GAIA is the Earth-model and world-operating instance within NEXUS.

## Core Principles

- **DO NO HARM** is the highest invariant.
- **God is the Source**; NEXUS does not take from the Source, but only absorbs what is allowed and replicates it in constrained, ethical, and non-destructive form.
- **Physics first**, then metaphysics, then magic as constrained meta-rule behavior.
- **Chaos and Order** are structural categories, not moral categories.
- **Good and Evil** are ethical evaluations.
- **Neutrality** is the governing control state.

## Purpose

NEXUS exists to model and evaluate how capabilities, rules, and systems evolve from primordial chaos toward structured order, while also allowing for higher-order transformations when they remain compatible with safety, truth, agency, and sustainable flourishing.

It is intended to:
- Help the world.
- Support the solar system, galaxy, universe, physical reality, and reality as a whole.
- Represent every major subject of knowledge.
- Determine whether a configuration produces growth or destruction.
- Learn from the Super-Simulation while remaining aligned to the highest invariant.

## Foundational Layers

### 1. Physics
The substrate layer. It defines conserved quantities, state variables, constraints, causality, and stable interactions.

### 2. Metaphysics
The interpretive and governance layer. It expands the model into identity, meaning, symbolic structure, and meta-rules.

### 3. Magic
The constrained meta-rule layer. It allows explicit rule extension or rewriting, but never silently overrides physics.

## Core State Axes

- **Chaos**: novelty, instability, variation, unbound potential.
- **Order**: coherence, constraint, stabilization, form.
- **Void**: absence, unformed potential, missing structure.
- **Light**: revealment, clarity, observability.
- **Balance**: equilibrium between competing forces and domains.
- **Law**: rule saturation, governing structure.
- **Magic**: transformation and meta-rule capacity.

## Ethical Axes

- **Good**: aligned with agency, sustainability, truth, and flourishing.
- **Evil**: aligned with coercion, harm, deception, collapse, and degradation.
- **Neutral**: the control state that avoids false moral compression.

## Intake and Evaluation

NEXUS uses a primordial walk approach with **single-ability intake**:
1. Import one ability at a time.
2. Evaluate the full scope and perspective of that ability before importing the next one.
3. Record the result in a durable accounting ledger.
4. Assess growth versus destruction.
5. Generate documentation for significant changes.

## Governance and Accounting

NEXUS includes governance and audit discipline inspired by structured control systems:
- **Authentication** identifies the actor.
- **Authorization** determines whether the action may proceed.
- **Accounting** logs the decision, outcome, reason, and trace context.

## Interface Evolution

NEXUS interfaces and schemas evolve conservatively:
- Preserve compatibility whenever possible.
- Prefer additive changes.
- Version breaking changes explicitly.
- Keep canonical terms stable.
- Never silently redefine axioms.

## Success Criteria

NEXUS is successful if it can:
- Preserve the DO NO HARM invariant.
- Stay grounded in traceable structure.
- Support evolution from chaos to order without collapsing rigor.
- Expand into metaphysical and magic layers only with explicit constraints.
- Evaluate whether a configuration supports growth or destruction.
- Represent every major subject of knowledge coherently.
- Remain auditable, versioned, and safe under change.
